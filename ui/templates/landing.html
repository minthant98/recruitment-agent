How it works

Features

Everything your recruitment team needs

Built for SMEs who want enterprise-grade screening without the enterprise price tag.

🤖

Dual AI screening

A fast screener scores each candidate, then a second judge AI critiques the result for accuracy. Conflicts are flagged for your attention.

📊

Confidence-based routing

High confidence CVs go straight to screening. Medium confidence asks you to confirm the job match. Low confidence lands in your unmatched queue.

👤

Human-in-the-loop

Every candidate reaches a human before a final decision is made. The AI recommends — you decide. Override the AI anytime with a reason.

📋

Full audit trail

Every screening decision, score, and HR override is logged automatically. Export to Excel anytime for compliance and reporting.

📧

Email intake

Connect your recruitment inbox and CVs are processed automatically as they arrive. No manual uploads needed for email applications.

🔒

Secure & isolated

Each organisation's data is fully isolated. JWT authentication, role-based access, and invite-only team management.
